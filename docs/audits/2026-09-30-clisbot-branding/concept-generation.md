# Concept generation record

Date: 2026-09-30. Method: built-in `image_gen`, preview-only raster concept board.
Production assets and source components have not been replaced.

## Generation brief

One landscape comparison board, three equally sized columns, the same scale
and presentation for each direction:

- Heading: `clisbot / Brand directions`.
- Subheading: `Your AI workspace and bot for work and personal life.`
- A: `OPEN C`; one open, rounded C frame with a subtle speech tail; forest,
  mint, warm paper. Descriptor: `Choose your AI.`
- B: `ROUNDTABLE`; a balanced assembly of independent rounded parts around a
  common center; graphite, saffron, oat. Descriptor: `Bring your bots together.`
- C: `COMPANION`; a compact conversation shape with two eyes in negative space;
  cobalt, ink, pale blue. Descriptor: `A bot of your own.`
- Each column: large mark, lowercase `clisbot` wordmark, app icon, illustrative
  favicon sizes, monochrome reversal, and palette swatches.
- Grounding: office work, software engineering, own-provider/model choice,
  native specialist-bot groups, cross-device/chat use, managed Hosts and access.
- Constraints: no Paseo butterfly, no AI sparkle, no stock robot illustration,
  no neural diagram, simple silhouettes, professional but approachable.

The first result had excessive transparency/glow and poor contrast. It was
sent back for a focused presentation correction before selecting a review image.

## Correction prompt

```text
Edit this brand comparison board for legibility and flat presentation. Preserve exactly the three columns, concept names A OPEN C, B ROUNDTABLE, C COMPANION, logo geometries, layout, clisbot spelling, icon thumbnails and palette values. The current image has unwanted transparency and smoky gradient/glow over the whole page. REMOVE ALL TRANSPARENCY: fully opaque solid warm-white #FAF9F5 canvas from edge to edge, every pixel alpha 255. All headings, wordmarks, explanatory labels and captions should be crisp DARK INK #172033 at full opacity, easily readable. Column A's large glyph is solid dark forest #12392F; B large glyph is solid saffron #D6AA42; C large glyph is solid cobalt #315CDD with WHITE eye cutouts clearly visible. Preserve app icons in their strong concept colors. Preserve monochrome strips. Replace every gradient, glow, haze, bloom, vignette and shadow with FLAT SOLID FILLS. No translucent anything, no dark overall backdrop. This should look like a clean printed Swiss graphic-design identity comparison sheet on opaque ivory paper, with hairline vertical dividers. Upper heading and all descriptors must have high contrast. Same three concepts and shapes; only correct backgrounds, opacity, flat colors and text readability. This is an OPAQUE page, NOT a transparent-background asset.
```
